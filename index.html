About Me

Hi, I’m Samantha! I’m a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, small business owner, and IT professional who genuinely enjoys figuring out why something isn’t working—and probably won’t leave it alone until I know why.

My background is a bit of a mix, but that’s one of my favorite things about it. I’ve worked in military environments, customer service, business operations, and technology, and I currently run my own e-commerce business while continuing my education in IT, networking, and cybersecurity. Each experience has taught me something different about problem-solving, communication, leadership, and staying calm when technology decides it has other plans.

I have a B.B.A. and an A.S. in Information Technology, and I’m continuing to develop my technical skills through hands-on training and certification preparation. My current areas of focus include networking, cybersecurity, system administration, troubleshooting, data analysis, and automation. I’m also developing my programming skills with Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Git, and GitHub—which is part of the reason this website exists in the first place.

What interests me most about technology is understanding what’s happening behind the scenes. I enjoy analyzing network traffic, troubleshooting unusual behavior, working through technical problems, and finding solutions that make technology work better for the people using it. I’m comfortable saying, “I don’t know yet,” because figuring out the yet part is usually the fun part.

Outside of technology, I’m a creative person and an entrepreneur. Running Mystic Dragon Keep has given me plenty of opportunities to combine creativity with the less-glamorous realities of managing a large inventory, maintaining accurate records, analyzing information, solving problems, and keeping an online business moving.

I’m currently preparing for the next chapter of my career and relocating to the Orlando, Florida area in June 2027, with flexibility to relocate sooner for the right opportunity. I’m looking for an opportunity where I can keep learning, contribute to a strong team, solve interesting problems, and put my growing collection of tech knowledge to work.

And yes, I built this website myself. If something breaks, I suppose that just means I’ve found my next troubleshooting project.
